Tweedie GLM of western rock lobster catch per pot
Abrolhos Marine Park, Yamatji Shallow Bank, 2025 and 2026
====================================================================== 

Formula: <response> ~ status * year + depth_z  
Family:  tweedie(link = "log")
Responses: n_legal, n_sublegal, n_legal_male, n_legal_female, n_male_large, n_total 
Pots used: 279 of 281 

NOTE: the legal male, legal female and large male models are subsets of
the legal model, not independent tests of it. The all lobsters model is the
legal and sublegal responses added together, so it is not independent either.

NOTE: the (1 | site) random effect specified by the supervisor is NOT
included, because string identifiers are not recorded in either survey.
Standard errors and p-values are therefore anti-conservative.

NOTE: status is spatially confounded with survey area. The National Park
Zone is a single contiguous block sampled on its own day in both years, so
'inside vs outside' is partly 'middle area vs north and south areas'.
